STILES ROX GYM WEBSITE
======================

Open index.html in a browser.

Included:
- Responsive modern single-page gym/fitness website
- STILES ROX metallic 3D badge/logo
- Personal training photos from the user's saved assets
- CSS 3D hover effects and tilt interactions
- Animated counters, scroll reveals, marquee and floating logo
- Training, mindset, journey, gallery and special contact sections
- WhatsApp and email contact links
- Contact form opens the visitor's email application (no backend required)

To publish: upload the whole STILES_ROX_Gym_Website folder to cPanel/Netlify/GitHub Pages or another static host.
